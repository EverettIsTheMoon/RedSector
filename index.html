RED SECTOR

Something is loose in the facility. Find it before it finds you.

VR: press ENTER VR below.
Controllers: left stick = move, right stick = turn, gun trigger = fire, A/B/X/Y or grip = reload, other trigger = flashlight.
Hand tracking: pinch index = fire, pinch middle = reload. Flashlight hand: fist = walk, pinch = light on/off. Ring-finger pinch = snap turn (left hand turns left, right hand turns right).
Swap gun hand: touch the glowing panel above your left wrist with your right finger (or press G).
The screen on the gun is a motion tracker. Listen: it is positional audio.

Desktop test: click the screen. WASD, mouse, click to fire, R reload, F flashlight, G swap hands, Enter restart.
